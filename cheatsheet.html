Cube Solve — Quick Reference

A memory jogger for while you're holding the cube — not a teaching page. If a step doesn't make sense, go back to the full tutorial for the explanation and animation.

Notation

A letter alone = quarter turn clockwise (viewed from that face). ' (prime) = quarter turn counter-clockwise. 2 = half turn (180°) — direction doesn't matter.

Standard Hold

White on the bottom, yellow on top — for Stages 1 through 6. Flip the whole cube only once, at the start of Stage 7 (white ends up on top, yellow on bottom). Never rotate the whole cube at any other point — only the layer named in the formula.

Golden Rules

  • Only turn the face(s) named in the current formula — rotating the whole cube mid-stage loses your bearings and undoes nothing you actually meant to fix.
  • Between Stage 7 corners, turn only the bottom (D) layer to bring in the next one — never the top, never the whole cube.
  • Run each formula the full number of times a stage calls for, then stop and re-check — running it further after it's solved just scrambles it again.
  • One flipped edge, one twisted corner, or one swapped pair by itself means the cube was taken apart and put back together wrong — no formula fixes that. See the "Impossible Cube?" card in the full tutorial for how to recover.
